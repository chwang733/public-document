88 Buddhas Privacy Policy / 88 Buddhas 隱私權政策

Effective date: September 27, 2026

English

88 Buddhas does not collect data from the app. It has no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking. We do not receive your practice information or camera images or share them with advertisers or analytics providers.

The app keeps your settings and any current practice or meditation session on your device so you can resume it. It does not keep a history of completed sessions. If you use the paired Apple Watch app, your devices may exchange your language setting and current practice status through Apple's WatchConnectivity service. We do not receive this information.

Camera-Guided Pacing is optional. When you use it, camera images are processed on your device to follow your movement. They are not recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. You can turn off camera access in your device settings.

To remove information stored by the app, delete 88 Buddhas from each device where it is installed. There is no account or server-held app data to delete. If you contact us by email, we use your message only to respond to you.

For privacy questions, contact sit_9verb@icloud.com. We will update this page if the app's privacy practices change.

繁體中文

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 27 日

88 Buddhas 不會從 App 收集資料。App 無須帳號，沒有廣告、分析或追蹤功能。我們不會取得您的練習資訊或相機影像，也不會與廣告或分析服務提供者分享這些資訊。

App 會將您的設定及目前進行中的禮佛或禪修保存在裝置上，方便您繼續練習。App 不保留已完成練習的歷史紀錄。如果您使用已配對的 Apple Watch App，您的裝置可能透過 Apple 的 WatchConnectivity 服務交換語言設定及目前練習狀態。我們不會取得這些資訊。

「相機引導節奏」是選用功能。使用時，相機影像只在您的裝置上處理，以辨識您的動作；影像不會被錄製、儲存或傳送。您可以在裝置設定中關閉相機權限。

若要移除 App 儲存的資訊，請從所有已安裝 88 Buddhas 的裝置刪除 App。我們沒有可供刪除的帳號或伺服器端 App 資料。若您以電子郵件聯絡我們，我們只會使用您的訊息回覆您。

如有隱私相關問題，請聯絡 sit_9verb@icloud.com。如果 App 的隱私處理方式改變，我們會更新本頁。
