# 88 Buddhas copyright draft

## App Store Connect copyright field

Enter this **without** a copyright symbol; Apple adds the symbol automatically:

```text
2026 Chihchung Wang
```

This assumes Chihchung Wang owns the exclusive rights to the app and first obtained those rights in 2026. Change the name or year if either assumption is incorrect.
